# Activon Embroidery – SEO Keywords & Hashtag Kit

> **Honest note:** no website can be *guaranteed* the #1 Google position. This site is built
> to rank well (fast, mobile-first, unique titles/descriptions, structured data, sitemap,
> internal links, keyword-rich content). Rankings then grow with: real product photos,
> Google Business/Search Console setup, regular new content, and backlinks (directories,
> B2B marketplaces, social profiles, supplier listings).
>
> **Hashtags do not affect Google rankings.** They help on Instagram, TikTok, Facebook,
> LinkedIn and Pinterest. Use them in social posts that link back to the website.
